Blockbuilt privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Blockbuilt keeps everything you enter on your iPhone. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not collect any data about you.

Who operates Blockbuilt

Blockbuilt is operated by Robert McIlwrick Carlson, an individual developer. For privacy questions, contact robertmcilwrick+blockbuiltapp@gmail.com.

Information stored in the app

Blockbuilt stores your training profile (goal, experience, age range, days and minutes, equipment, injuries and exercises to avoid), your routines, logged sets, maxes, optional body-weight entries and settings in local app storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third party. Blockbuilt makes no network requests with your data.

Prompts and outside AI tools

Blockbuilt can copy a training prompt, built from your profile, to the clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste that prompt into an AI service or other app yourself. If you do, that service handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy. When you paste a reply back, Blockbuilt reads it on your iPhone only. Your profile may include injuries or limitations you typed in, so review the prompt before you share it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Blockbuilt schedules a local reminder on your iPhone when a rest timer ends. No notification data leaves your device.

Backups and CSV exports

When you export a CSV file or a full backup, iOS lets you choose where to save or share it. The place you choose handles the file under its own terms. An exported copy stays there until you delete it. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup may also include Blockbuilt's data, as with any app, under Apple's terms.

Children

Blockbuilt does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Retention and your choices

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies you exported remain where you saved them.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if Blockbuilt's data practices change; the effective date above will show the latest version. For questions, email robertmcilwrick+blockbuiltapp@gmail.com.

iPhone app

Build the block. Earn the weight.

Blockbuilt writes an expert training prompt from your goal, equipment, time and injuries. Paste it into any AI, paste the answer back, and you have a 4-week program. Log your sets and Blockbuilt works out what to lift next time.

What it does

  • Strength, muscle, power, conditioning and sport goals, including deadline goals.
  • Supersets, substitutes with matching sets and reps, and a rest timer.
  • Swappable progression rules that set your next weight from the sets you log.
  • Progress charts and an estimated max for every lift.
  • No account, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone, with CSV export.

Build a program

  1. Open Build and fill in your goal, experience, days, minutes, equipment and any injuries or exercises to avoid.
  2. Tap Copy prompt and paste it into any AI chat you use.
  3. Copy the AI's whole reply, come back, and tap Paste from clipboard. Check the preview and tap Save routine.

If the import fails, make sure you copied the entire reply, including the block that starts with program_json. If the reply was cut off, ask the AI to continue or to send the JSON again.

Logging and weights

Enter the weight and reps for each set and tap the circle to start the rest timer. Tap Finish workout to save. Blockbuilt uses your progression rule (Settings, Weight rules) to suggest your next weight. Set the smallest weight jump your gym allows, and add any known maxes so your first weights are not guesses. Turn on the find-your-weights workout to work up to your starting loads.

Backup and moving phones

Blockbuilt has no account, so your data lives only on your iPhone. In Settings, Backup, use Export full backup to save everything to Files or iCloud Drive, and Import CSV or backup on your new phone. Export history (CSV) gives you every set in a spreadsheet.

Safety

Blockbuilt is a training tool, not medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat injuries. If you have an injury, a health condition or are pregnant, talk to a doctor or physical therapist before training. Stop and get help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, faintness, or sharp or worsening pain. Blockbuilt covers training only; it gives no diet or nutrition advice.
